Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Pocketfile does not collect your data. Your folders, notes, photos, recordings and tasks stay on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We can't see any of it. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account to sign up for.

What we collect

Nothing. Pocketfile has no servers of its own and doesn't send your content, your usage or any identifier to us or to anyone else.

Where your content is stored

  • On your iPhone. Everything you create is saved on your device.
  • In your iCloud, if you use it. When iCloud sync is on, your content is stored in your private iCloud account so it syncs between your devices. Apple runs that storage under the Apple Privacy Policy. We can't access it.

Permissions Pocketfile asks for

Each one is optional, and you can change it any time in the iPhone Settings app.

  • Camera: to take photos and scan documents into your folders.
  • Location (while using the app): to save where a photo was taken and to fill in a folder's address. You can turn off saving photo locations in Pocketfile's settings.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for dictation and the voice recordings you make. Recordings are turned into text on your iPhone.
  • Photos: to save photos you choose to your Photos library.
  • Notifications: for task reminders you set.

Apple services the app uses

  • Apple Maps: when you search for an address or open a location, that request goes to Apple.
  • In-app purchases: Apple processes all payments. We never see your payment details.

Links and websites

When you add a link, Pocketfile may load that page to show its title and image. When you open a link, the page loads inside the app. In both cases the website receives a normal web request from your device and may handle it under its own privacy policy.

Children

Pocketfile isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To remove synced copies, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
