Common questions

Where is my stuff stored?On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if iCloud is turned on. Nothing is stored on our servers. We don't have any.
I deleted something by mistake.Open Recently Deleted. Deleted items stay there for 30 days before they're removed for good.
Pocketfile isn't saving where a photo was taken.Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Pocketfile on your iPhone, and make sure it's set to While Using the App.
The camera or microphone doesn't work.Go to Settings › Apps › Pocketfile on your iPhone and turn on Camera or Microphone.

When you email

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot is great too.
